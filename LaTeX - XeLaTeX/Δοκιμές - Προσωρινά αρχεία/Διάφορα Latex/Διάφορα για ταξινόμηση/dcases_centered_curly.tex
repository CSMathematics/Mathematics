\section*{\texttt{dcases*} in \texttt{align} work?}
\verb|dcases*| and friends from \verb|mathtools| work,
iff \verb|curlybraces| is set.
\subsection*{Package \texttt{mtpro2} options: \texttt{lite,\bracesshape}}

\begin{sympycode}
from sympy import *
x = symbols('x')
myeq = Eq(x**2 + 3*x - 10)
s=solve(myeq,dict=True)
\end{sympycode}
The roots of the equation $\sympy{myeq}$
are $x=\sympy{s[0][x]}$ and $x=\sympy{s[1][x]}$.

\lysh{16428_SOLUTION}{1}
ΛΥΣΗ

Έχουμε: $|\vec{\alpha}| = \frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}$ (1), $|\vec{\beta}| = \frac{1}{2}$ (2) και $|3\vec{\alpha} + 2\vec{\beta}| = |\vec{\alpha} - 2\vec{\beta}|$ (3).

\text{α)} Από την (3)
\[ |3\vec{\alpha} + 2\vec{\beta}|^2 = |\vec{\alpha} - 2\vec{\beta}|^2 \Leftrightarrow \]
\[ (3\vec{\alpha} + 2\vec{\beta})^2 = (\vec{\alpha} - 2\vec{\beta})^2 \Leftrightarrow \]
\[ 9\vec{\alpha}^2 + 12\vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\beta} + 4\vec{\beta}^2 = \vec{\alpha}^2 - 4\vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\beta} + 4\vec{\beta}^2 \Leftrightarrow \]
\[ 16\vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\beta} = -8\vec{\alpha}^2 \Leftrightarrow \vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\beta} = -\frac{1}{2}\vec{\alpha}^2 = -\frac{1}{2}|\vec{\alpha}|^2. \]
Η τελευταία σχέση, λόγω της (1) δίνει:
\[ \vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\beta} = -\frac{1}{2} \cdot \left(\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}\right)^2 = -\frac{1}{2} \cdot \frac{3}{4} = -\frac{3}{8}. \]

\text{β)} Επίσης:
\[ \sigma\upsilon\nu(\widehat{\vec{\alpha}, \vec{\beta}}) = \frac{\vec{\alpha} \cdot \vec{\beta}}{|\vec{\alpha}| \cdot |\vec{\beta}|}. \]
Η τελευταία ισότητα λόγω του ερωτήματος (\text{α}) και των (1), (2) δίνει:
\[ \sigma\upsilon\nu(\widehat{\vec{\alpha}, \vec{\beta}}) = \frac{-\frac{3}{8}}{\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2} \cdot \frac{1}{2}} = -\frac{3 \cdot 4}{8 \cdot \sqrt{3}} = -\frac{3}{2\sqrt{3}} = -\frac{\sqrt{3}}{2}, \]
άρα $\widehat{(\vec{\alpha}, \vec{\beta})} = \frac{5\pi}{6}$ ή $150^\circ$.
\end{lysh}

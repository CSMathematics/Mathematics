\lysh{15186_SOLUTION}{1}
ΛΥΣΗ
α) Αν $M(x_M, y_M)$ και $N(x_N, y_N)$, είναι
\[
x_M = \frac{6+2}{2} = 4, \quad y_M = \frac{3+1}{2} = 2
\]
και
\[
x_N = \frac{9+1}{2} = 5, \quad y_N = \frac{2-2}{2} = 0.
\]
β) Είναι $\vec{MN} = (5-4, 0-2) = (1,-2)$ και $\vec{\Delta\Gamma} = (9-1, 2-(-2)) = (8,4).$
γ) Έχουμε ότι:
\[
\vec{MN} \cdot \vec{\Delta\Gamma} = (1,-2) \cdot (8,4) = 1 \cdot 8 + (-2) \cdot 4 = 8 - 8 = 0.
\]
Άρα, $\vec{MN} \perp \vec{\Delta\Gamma}.$

ΘΕΜΑ 2

Δίνεται τρίγωνο ΑΒΓ τέτοιο, ώστε ΑΓ = 2ΑΒ. Η διχοτόμος του ΑΔ τέμνει την διάμεσο ΒΕ στο σημείο Ζ. Να αποδείξετε ότι:

\begin{alist}
\item ΑΒ = ΑΕ = $\frac{Α\Gamma}{2}$. \monades{7}

\item ΔΒ = ΔΕ. \monades{8}

\item $A Z \perp B E$ \monades{10}


\begin{center}
\begin{tikzpicture}
\tkzDefPoint(0,5){A}
\tkzDefPoint(-2,0){B}
\tkzDefPoint(4,0){\Gamma}
\tkzDefMidPoint(A,G_pt)\tkzGetPoint{E}
\tkzDefLine[bisector](B,A,G_pt)\tkzGetPoint{a_dir}
\tkzInterLL(A,a_dir)(B,G_pt)\tkzGetPoint{\Delta}
\tkzInterLL(A,D_pt)(B,E)\tkzGetPoint{Z}
\draw[line width=0.3mm] (A)--(B)--(G_pt)--cycle;
\draw[line width=0.3mm] (A)--(D_pt);
\draw[line width=0.3mm] (B)--(E);
\tkzDrawPoints(A,B,G_pt,D_pt,E,Z)
\tkzLabelPoint[above](A){$A$}
\tkzLabelPoint[left](B){$B$}
\tkzLabelPoint[right](G_pt){$\Gamma$}
\tkzLabelPoint[below](D_pt){$\Delta$}
\tkzLabelPoint[right](E){$E$}

\tkzMarkAngle[size=0.6,mark=none](D_pt,A,B)

\tkzLabelAngle[pos=0.8](E,A,D_pt){$2$}
\end{tikzpicture}
\end{center}
\end{alist}
